The Loki Scarf

Difficulty: Sleepy Cat (Easy)

Keep Your Feline Toasty

Every cat deserves a splash of color. This pattern uses my signature teal and orange palette to create a scarf that is as warm as a sunbeam on a rug.

Materials

  • 50g DK weight yarn in 'Loki Teal'
  • 20g DK weight yarn in 'Tangerine Dream'
  • 4mm knitting needles
  • Darning needle for those messy ends
  • A very patient cat

Measurements

Width: 4cm (perfect for tucking under whiskers)

Length: 45cm (adjustable based on neck floof)

Gauge: 22 sts = 10cm in garter stitch

The Instructions

1

Cast on 10 stitches using the Teal yarn. We use a simple long-tail cast on because it is stretchy—like a cat stretching after a nap!

2

Knit every row (Garter Stitch) for 10 rows. Then, switch to the Orange yarn. Don't worry about the tails yet, Pip will help watch them.

3

Continue alternating colors every 10 rows until the scarf reaches 40cm. Finish with a final 5cm of Teal yarn.

4

Cast off loosely. For the fringe: cut 10cm lengths of both colors and loop them through the ends. Trim so it looks neat!
