Frost-Moth Midnight Cocoa

A brew for quiet paws and winter dreams

The Elements

  • 2 cups whole oat milk
  • 3 tbsp dark cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 1 star anise pod
  • A pinch of sea salt
  • A dash of cinnamon

The Ritual

  1. In a small heavy-bottomed pot, whisk together the cocoa powder and cinnamon with a tiny splash of milk to form a smooth paste.
  2. Slowly pour in the remaining milk, whisking constantly to ensure no lumps remain.
  3. Add the star anise and maple syrup. Heat gently over medium-low heat. Do not let it boil—we want a slow, cozy warmth.
  4. Once steaming, stir in the vanilla and sea salt. The salt wakes up the chocolate!
  5. Remove the star anise, pour into your thickest ceramic mug, and sit near a window.

Pip's Mouse-Tip: Hover here to reveal the secret ingredient...

Add a tiny square of orange-infused dark chocolate at the very bottom of the cup before pouring. It's like a little sunset in your drink!
