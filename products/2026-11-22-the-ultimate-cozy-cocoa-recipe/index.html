Instructions

The Paste: In your favorite mug, whisk the cocoa, maple syrup, salt, and a splash of warm milk until a smooth, shiny paste forms.
The Warmth: Gently heat the rest of the milk in a small pan until tiny bubbles appear around the edges. Do not let it boil!
The Pour: Slowly pour the hot milk into the mug, stirring constantly to keep the texture silky.
The Finish: Stir in the vanilla, top with marshmallows, and use a grater to add a tiny dusting of extra cocoa on top.
Pip's Mouse-Sized Secret: If you want it extra frothy, use a small hand-held milk frother for 10 seconds right before adding the marshmallows. It makes the cocoa light like a cloud!
