The Recipe: Loki's "Meowmellow" Cocoa

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk (or oat milk!)
  • 50g dark chocolate, chopped small
  • 1 tbsp high-quality cocoa powder
  • A pinch of flaky sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon (the secret!)

Instructions

1. Heat milk in a small pot until tiny bubbles appear.
2. Whisk in cocoa powder and cinnamon until smooth.
3. Add chocolate chunks and stir until melted and glossy.
4. Pour into your favorite mug, add salt, and top with far too many marshmallows.
