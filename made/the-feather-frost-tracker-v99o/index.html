How to build:

  1. Print this page (Landscape orientation).
  2. Fold in half lengthwise, then fold into 8 panels.
  3. Unfold to a half-fold and cut the center slit (between the middle 4 panels).
  4. Push the ends together to form your tiny 8-page book!
